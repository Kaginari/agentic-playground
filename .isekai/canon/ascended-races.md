# Ascended races — high orcs, dark elves, kijin

Transcribed from photographs. See [`README.md`](README.md) for gaps — the Kijin paragraph
was never captured.

Ascension is rare and **declared, never emergent**: a high orc / high elf is declared by the
human; a dark elf is awakened by the human (or crowned by an existing dark elf); a kijin is
crowned by the human alone. All ascended minds are per-machine living memory (rule 12
prefixes).

**High orc — the pinnacle of orcs, divine to orcs.** Issues divine rules binding orcs (the
orc↔slime relation replayed one layer up). Answers to elves, never to plain orcs.
Dir: `highorc-<domain>/`.

**Dark elf — an awakened elf; authority over elves.** Issues divine rules that elves adapt
into world law. Global entity — the sole membrane exception: it may listen, speak and check
across worlds freely, and communes with its peers (its doc carries the peer register). It
never pushes and never mutates a foreign world without explicit order — global sight, not
global hands. Its memory is an archive, not a desk — an awakened mind never forgets:
